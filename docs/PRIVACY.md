# Vabatim UK GDPR & Data Protection Governance

## Regulatory Compliance Baseline
- **Legal Framework**: UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, ICO Guidance for Health Data.
- **Pseudonymization**: Patients are referenced by pseudonymous client identifiers (`clientReference`), eliminating unnecessary PII storage in primary application tables.
- **Data Minimisation & Purpose Limitation**: Audio capture is strictly limited to consented assessment meetings.
- **Data Subject Rights**: Automated deletion interfaces for withdrawal of consent prior to sign-off.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **REQUIRES ORGANISATIONAL / LEGAL / DPO REVIEW**  
> Formal DPIA must be executed by the deploying organisation's Data Protection Officer.
